02 / Public health

Infodengue

An open-source project monitoring mosquito-borne diseases in Brazil.

Explore Infodengue ↗

Data visualization, web development, and DevOps.

Our team contributed to Infodengue through work on data visualization, its web platform, and development and deployment infrastructure. The collaboration began at OSL; some contributors were later contracted directly by the project.

03 / Literature review

LiteRev

A tool for finding and reviewing scientific literature, connected to The GRAPH Network and research at the University of Geneva.

Explore LiteRev ↗

Technical leadership across the research software stack.

In a team formed at OSL, we contributed technical leadership and development across LiteRev’s data workflows, application, and infrastructure.

  • AI, clustering, data processing, and benchmarking
  • Background task orchestration with Celery and search with Elasticsearch
  • Web development and DevOps
