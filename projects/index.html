03 / Literature review

LiteRev

A scientific literature review project connected to The GRAPH Network. Its University of Geneva founding team includes Erol Orel, Fatma Aziza Merzouki, and Professor Olivia Keiser.

More about LiteRev ↗

Research data workflows, search, and software infrastructure.

Contributions included technical leadership, data processing, clustering, AI-related work, benchmarking, background tasks with Celery, search with Elasticsearch, web development, and DevOps.
