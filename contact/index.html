01 / Start a project

Tell us the challenge.
Let's find a path.

Need help with statistical analysis, scientific computing, or research software? Share what you're working on. You don't need a formal brief or finalized requirements to start a conversation.

Email SciStitch ↗ connect@scistitch.com

A useful first message

Help us understand your project.

  1. 01

    The challenge

    What question, problem, or goal would you like to address?

  2. 02

    Current context

    Tell us about your organization, available data, methods, existing software, or relevant public links.

  3. 03

    Practical constraints

    If known, share your timeline, preferred deliverables, and approximate budget. These can be discussed later.

Please avoid sending confidential, sensitive, or personal datasets in your first email. We can discuss appropriate ways to share data if a project moves forward.

What happens next

We can discuss fit, scope, availability, and an appropriate next step. Any project proposal and contracting arrangement would be agreed separately before work begins.
