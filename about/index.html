01 / Purpose & values

Make knowledge
work in practice.

Our purpose is to help people turn scientific knowledge and technical ideas into useful, lasting tools.

Our mission is to build reliable software and data solutions with research teams, businesses, and open-source communities, sharing the knowledge they need to use and maintain them.

[ 01 ] Evidence

Scientific rigor

Question assumptions, test our work, and make methods and limitations clear.

[ 02 ] Openness

Open collaboration

Share reusable work when project agreements allow, communicate openly, and credit contributions.

[ 03 ] Longevity

Sustainable engineering

Favor maintainable solutions, useful documentation, and choices that fit the team’s resources.

[ 04 ] People

Inclusion & respect

Welcome different perspectives and consider accessibility in how we work and what we build.

[ 05 ] Care

Responsible technology

Handle data with care and consider privacy, risks, and the effects of software and AI on people.

[ 06 ] Learning

Shared capability

Learn together and transfer knowledge so collaborators can understand, use, and extend the work.

+

Our vision is a future where research and technology are easier to understand, reuse, and build on.

02 / Our story

From collaboration.
To SciStitch.

SciStitch grew from research software collaborations at Open Science Labs.

Members of our team previously contributed to earlier phases of projects including Infodengue and LiteRev, alongside open-source tools. That shared experience helped shape SciStitch.
