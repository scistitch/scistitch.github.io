01 / Our story

Real projects.
Lasting connections.

Research collaborations and support for OSL’s wider community shaped our team before SciStitch became independent.

Explore our contributions ↗
  1. 01

    Supporting open research

    Infodengue

    An early OSL team supported Infodengue, an open-source project monitoring mosquito-borne diseases in Brazil. Some contributors were later contracted directly by the project.

    Explore Infodengue ↗ About the project ↗
  2. 02

    Building another team

    LiteRev

    OSL then formed another team to work on LiteRev, a tool for finding and reviewing scientific literature.

    LiteRev is connected to The GRAPH Network (Global Research and Analyses for Public Health) and research at the University of Geneva (UNIGE).

    Explore LiteRev ↗ The GRAPH Network ↗ LiteRev at UNIGE ↗
  3. 03

    Supporting the wider community

    OSL Incubator & initiatives

    Alongside these research collaborations, we supported the OSL Incubator Program through development and maintenance of open-source tools, including Makim, Sugar, and PyMedX.

    We also created and maintained OSL’s technical infrastructure, from bots and continuous integration to websites, plugins, and DevOps workflows. Our support extended to other OSL initiatives and collaborations with some of its partners.

    OSL Incubator Program ↗ Our OSL contributions ↗
  4. 04

    Becoming independent

    SciStitch

    This shared experience became the foundation for SciStitch, an independent consulting organization formed by the research software engineering team.

    OSL can call on SciStitch for external consulting and development requests.
