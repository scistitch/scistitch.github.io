04

Fiscal support for joint initiatives.

Eligible joint initiatives may arrange fiscal support through OSL. Open Source Collective hosts OSL’s open-source work; formal OSL agreements require its review and approval.

OSL also works with The GRAPH Network, represented by ASCRES, for non-open-source financial administration. Confirm the host and contracting route with OSL for each initiative. Independent SciStitch contracts follow separate arrangements.
