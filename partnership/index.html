01 / Our partner

SciStitch. × Open Science Labs ↗

A shared purpose.
A wider circle.

SciStitch is a consulting and open-source development partner of Open Science Labs.

We contribute engineering expertise and a practical understanding of scientific software. OSL connects that work with a global community of contributors, mentors, and organizations working toward more open science and technology.

Through this partnership, we can explore joint projects, exchange knowledge with OSL’s partners, and work with its team to arrange fiscal-host support for eligible initiatives.

02 / What the partnership makes possible

A wider community.
A stronger connection.

Our partnership with OSL connects SciStitch to people, organizations, and shared resources beyond our own team. It gives us more ways to exchange knowledge, find collaborators, and develop joint initiatives.

Discover OSL’s partnership network ↗
01

Collaborators across borders.

OSL brings together researchers, developers, maintainers, mentors, and newcomers across countries and disciplines. Through this community, we can exchange perspectives and explore collaborations with people whose experience complements our own.

Its Ambassadors Program is designed to strengthen connections between local communities, universities, organizations, and the wider OSL network.

Meet the community ↗Explore the Ambassadors Program ↗
02

Connections across organizations.

OSL’s published partner network includes The GRAPH Network, pyOpenSci, and Data Umbrella, connecting work across research, education, scientific software, and data science. Our relationship with OSL creates opportunities to explore introductions and shared initiatives with its partners.

Explore OSL’s partners ↗
03

Knowledge that travels further.

OSL’s Community Partners hub shares independently organized learning opportunities and welcomes workshops, resources, and mentoring initiatives. It gives us a place to propose activities, share experience, and connect useful work with people who can learn from it and contribute to it.

Discover community initiatives ↗
04

Fiscal support for joint initiatives.

Through OSL, eligible joint initiatives can access fiscal-host support to receive funds, manage payments, and formalize agreements. Open Source Collective hosts OSL’s open-source work; formal OSL engagements follow its review and approval process.

For non-open-source initiatives, OSL also works with The GRAPH Network, represented by ASCRES, on financial administration. We agree the appropriate host and contracting route with OSL for each collaboration. Independently contracted SciStitch work follows its own arrangements.

03 / How OSL’s community works

A place to learn.
A community to build with.

Discover OSL’s mission ↗

Open Science Labs is a global community connecting newcomers, researchers, developers, and maintainers around open science and technology. It helps people turn learning into practical experience, with mentorship, a sense of belonging, and recognition for their contributions. Its principles of open collaboration and inclusion guide work intended to benefit research, education, and society.

[ 01 ] Learn

Mentorship & learning

Mentorship and structured onboarding help newcomers contribute to real open-source projects. OSL’s OSS Internship and Research Seed programs connect practical experience with software development, including research ideas taking their first steps as open-source tools.

Explore contributor programs ↗
[ 02 ] Build

Project incubation

OSL helps early-stage projects, projects restarting development, and those moving to open source work toward sustainable maintenance. Incubation can include guidance on roadmaps, documentation, testing, releases, and contributor onboarding, shaped together with project maintainers.

Explore project incubation ↗
[ 03 ] Connect

Project affiliation

Affiliation connects independently maintained projects with OSL’s wider community. Projects can gain visibility, exchange knowledge, and explore contributor programs while keeping their own governance and technical direction. It creates room for collaboration across projects and disciplines.

Explore project affiliation ↗
